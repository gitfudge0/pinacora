Reframed's first public release brings the live Reframed gallery to a native macOS wallpaper browser built with Rust and GPUI.

Browse artwork shelves, search loaded titles and artists, inspect attribution, and apply the untouched full resolution original to every currently connected display. Includes a welcome walkthrough, replayable Guide, a Motion toggle, and persistent preview/original caches. No gallery artwork is bundled.

### Downloads

- **Apple Silicon:** `Reframed-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip`
- **Intel:** `Reframed-0.1.0-macos-x86_64.zip`
- SHA-256 checksum files accompany the archives.

Requires **macOS 12 or newer** and internet access for the live gallery. Unzip the archive for your Mac and move Reframed.app to Applications.

These bundles are **ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple**. If macOS blocks opening and you trust the release, attempt to open it, then use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (macOS 12: System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General). Do not bypass a malware or damaged-app warning; report it.

### Known limitations

The app depends on public gallery pages and CDN access. Automatic updates, wallpaper rotation, launch at login, automatic cache-size caps, and wallpaper management for other Spaces/disconnected displays are not included. Disable motion using the in-app toggle when needed.

This is an unofficial app, independent of and not endorsed by Reframed. Artwork rights remain with their respective holders.
